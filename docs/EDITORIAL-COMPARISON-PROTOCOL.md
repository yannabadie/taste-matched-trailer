# Author choice: delivered edit workbench and an unrun comparison

The immediate task is to choose an editing direction before spending time on a
trailer. This is a product hypothesis; no author demand, preference or time saving
has been established. A trailer may be the wrong marketing investment for a book.

## Delivered change, separate from engine 2.6.3

The static frontend offers two complete local edits of an accepted four-shot
result: **Let the images lead** and **Let the words lead**. It uses the source's
actual images, captions, narration, shot order, assets and reference bindings.
The durations and voice entrance change; it adds no plot event, reveal, scene,
character or measured claim. This is a deterministic editing layer written by
Codex, not another native Qwen generation and not a Qloo recommendation.
These are two cadences of one received scenario, not two different narrative
angles. The stronger recommendation to offer distinct author directions is
therefore only partly addressed; no claim of complete resolution is made.

The local text storyboard makes the contrast perceptible over time. Play either
cadence to see four actual received image descriptions and progressively revealed
received words, with an image hold before narration in the images-led edit.
Stop and replay operate locally and do not select an export. This is a reading
preview, not generated imagery, spoken audio, a video or a quality measurement.
Real wall-clock callback reception under an isolated DOM is kept separate from
the root operator's rendered-browser reception.

The same total duration, synopsis, spoiler rules and off-screen constraint remain
in both choices. A source fixing individual shot lengths is refused. Sound-off
plans contain no spoken or sonic track; existing narration may be moved verbatim
to captions. Missing words or a duration leaving no contrast are refused rather
than filled with generic mystery text. A gardening control exercises a non-mystery
canon. Long narration is preserved and flagged for timing review; it is never
silently accelerated or claimed to fit rendered audio. The estimates are declared
as 2.5 spoken words and 3 caption words per second, without a language guarantee.

The author may select either edit, a tie, or neither, and add a note. The separate
decision export carries the source hash, source date, original UTF-8 export and
document, alternatives, selected timing, sound state and limitations. Changing
inputs invalidates the local choice. An already downloaded decision is historical.
The ordinary original-download button and original writer review remain intact;
that review does not certify these recompositions.

The dated reader remains distinct from a new live analysis. Original creative
words keep their received language; English editing instructions do not translate
them. Neither a live result nor either edit is a rendered film.

## One useful constraint: adapt the received brief for sound-off viewing

The local sound-off tool is a separate task from choosing a cadence. It keeps
the original shot timing and images, displays the original narration and captions,
and identifies a spoken word sequence not reproduced on screen. This is a case-
and punctuation-insensitive text comparison, not proof that the image fails to
convey that information. The proposed screen text combines the received caption
and missing narration; no new plot sentence is invented by the transfer.

The author can correct a specific plan's screen text, then explicitly accept or
reject the local adaptation. Free text is attributed as an unverified author
correction. An edit to text or the decision note invalidates the previous local
decision. The native parent's `user_constraints` and verdict remain unchanged.
The local delivery constraint `sound_off: true` belongs only to the derivative.

The derivative uses its own schema and `local-silent-<SHA256>` identity. Its hash
covers canonical payload JSON including parent identity/hash, exact captions,
decision, note, original timing, local checks and explicit verification limits.
It attaches the exact original UTF-8 bytes and document separately. Native
`verification`, `qloo_api_verified` and `constraint_fidelity` are not copied into
the derivative. Historical Qloo reference links are labelled parent lineage.
Native variant verification is `not_executed`; canon/spoilers are `not_verified`;
understandability still requires author review, including for an accepted local
decision with a favourable word-rate estimate.

Fixed individual timing is kept and warned about. Long or missing text is shown
as a constraint issue; missing text prevents acceptance but its rejected draft can
still be exported. Parent changes, original-byte/hash mismatch, structural changes
or concurrent edits fail before a new exact decision/export is delivered. The
cadence preview continues to use the original words; corrected silent captions
are inspected in their editor and separate derivative export.

## Zero-family author task — NOT RUN

Use the same historical parent and the same sound-off delivery goal for three
anonymised documents: the original and the two local recompositions. Alternate
presentation order and give every reader the same constraints and inspection
time. Allow the original, either recomposition, a tie or none. Ask for one concrete
passage motivating the decision, the main remaining defect and the exact local
correction to keep in the sound-off export. Retain rejection for a canon/spoiler
or visibility problem ahead of aesthetic preference. Stop expansion if the
differences are decorative, need major rewriting or do not support a concrete
decision. This tests written-brief usefulness, not video perception or audience.
The historical original already uses Qloo; it is not a no-Qloo baseline.

## What is received without consuming the demonstration pool

`node --test tests/test_editorial_workbench.mjs` exercises the delivered source,
including browser choice/download logic under an isolated DOM. It does not invoke
Qloo, a model, an admission endpoint or a runtime process.

`scripts/replay-editorial-workbench.mjs` optionally consumes the operator's existing
private pinned recording. It verifies the original export bytes and writes a
network-disabled private preview and receipt outside the public repository.
Do not commit its outputs: the official provider data stays private. Scripted
choice exports are technical controls, not feedback from an author.

The eight frozen evaluation cases, previous failures, public pool and runtime
1.1.3 are unchanged. No fresh provider availability, live language compliance,
native generation or global application requalification follows from this delta.

## Proposed A/B/C comparison — NOT RUN

The objective is to separate the contribution of provider evidence from the
contribution of this application's selection, verification and author workflow.
Comparing only A and C would confound them.

| Condition | Same inputs and target | Difference supplied to the same model |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A: capable LLM without Qloo | Same book canon, author intention, public reference titles, shortlist, duration, assets, spoiler/audio/visibility constraints; request two complete usable directions | A strong creative instruction, without provider affinity evidence. Do not deliberately withhold the current brief or constraints. |
| B: same LLM with official Qloo kit | Same task and received cultural entities/results as C | Use the official kit correctly for resolution/ranking and make its evidence available; give a fair instruction to make and explain a choice. No intentionally stale or broken baseline. |
| C: this application | Same task, provider dataset and model configuration as B | Application selector and lineage, native verification, then the explicit local editing choice. Label that the second edit is a deterministic recomposition rather than a second model generation. |

Freeze model revision, supported effort, temperature/seed where exposed, prompts,
context limits, tool versions, candidate shortlist and maximum role/output budget
before running. A and B receive competent analysis/writing/checking opportunities;
C must not get extra information or hidden retries. If the route cannot expose or
match a parameter, preserve that limitation rather than asserting equality.

Use one development brief to settle the protocol, then a small held-out set with
at least a non-mystery book, a visibility/spoiler constraint and a deliberately
incompatible sound/timing requirement. These are new comparison cases, not edits
to the original eight. Do not replace a failed case with the positive recording.
For B/C reuse the same permitted, private dated evidence or receive one common
dataset; replay must be labelled historical and cannot qualify a fresh kit call.
The actual manuscript/canon is author-provided; affinities among public books and
films do not measure that unpublished manuscript or prove a best creative angle.

Authors inspect anonymised outputs in mixed order, under identical time and
materials. Record fidelity to canon, spoiler/visibility/audio compliance,
perceptible difference, feasibility, reference accuracy, and whether the author
can choose/use a direction. Retain verbatim reasons, ties, neither choices,
constraint failures, clarification, unsupported references and unavailable runs.
An app refusal is a feasibility outcome, not an automatic win over a useful LLM
answer. Report each case and B versus C as well as A versus B/C. Do not infer a
sales lift, probability of winning or general quality advantage from a small set.

## Cost and execution gate before any future pilot

Current authorised execution here is **zero** Qloo/model calls and zero new
families. The six unallocated demonstration families are not spent on mappings.
A prospective matched three-role A/B/C case would require up to nine native role
calls, before retries; exact token cost, available allowance and the valid route
for A/B have not been received. A replay of C plus new A/B is not a paired run.

Before one pilot, root must inspect the existing route, protected budget state,
licence-compliant evidence reuse and normal resource admission/lease, and receive
a concrete maximum reservation for all three conditions. Use no direct runner,
budget reset, alternate credentials, paid API or hidden retry. Preserve enough
allowance for jury access. If the matched route or cost cannot be received, keep
the workbench demonstration and mark A/B/C and author benefit **not evaluated**.

## Narrative the evidence currently supports

“Qloo informs the references in a received brief. I can inspect those references,
then compare two local edits of the same images and words and record my choice.
The original constraints, source date and original document stay visible. I have
not yet measured whether authors prefer this workflow or whether it beats a good
LLM with the official kit.”
