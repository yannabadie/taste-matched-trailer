purpose no longer requires a physical trace, and the visual delivery guard now
handles explicit negative nominal lists without suppressing affirmative clauses
elsewhere. The first framing candidate, first outputs, initial failures and

## Framing change and prospective test

The old `_shot_plan` unconditionally asked the writer to develop a material
trace. The new second purpose preserves the synopsis's degree of certainty:
a felt presence remains subjective; a physical clue is optional and must fit
the canon rather than establish an unprovided intrusion. Captions should make
the perception understandable without turning a feeling into a fact.

Three contrasts were fixed before outputs: perception alone; a real handprint
whose author, age and connection remain unknown; and subjective presence with
sound off and objects/typography only. Only the first synopsis is the authentic
original; the other two are synthetic contrasts. All reuse the same historical
cultural context. This is not three new cultural acquisitions.

The real `llm_role_2_creative_redactor` function constructed each input and the
actual `guarded_qwen_runner.build_messages` built SYSTEM and USER messages.
A capture callback stopped before generation. Per pair, only
`/shot_plan/1/purpose` differed; constraints, references, supplied analysis theme
and SYSTEM were identical. No Role1 inference was performed.

Six fresh Codex contexts received these exact messages through one common
minimal envelope, with maximum effort requested, no tools and one first answer
each. Order was randomized before production. Labels were masked for the
independent reader, but content could reveal provenance; this was not double
blind. Model selection inherited the parent; per-call provider/model/compute
metadata were not returned. Input lengths differ. The actual native schema and
field bounds were used: four complete shot overrides,240/140/160/90characters
for image/voice/sound/caption, with1200output tokens requested. This is separate
from the earlier500word/12caption-word pilot.

The Qwen route fixes `enable_thinking=False`, temperature0.2 and its received
caps. It is not maximum reasoning. Those settings were left unchanged, and
Codex responses are not a native Qwen, kit or full application execution.

## Distinct guard defect and countercheck

The guard rejected negative descriptions such as an explicit list excluding
people/hands/faces, or a sentence denying that an anomaly or figure appears.
The repair masks only explicitly negated nominal spans in a small EN/FR list
grammar. It does not exempt a whole sentence or shot because it contains
“no”, “pas” or “narrator”. Positive people/narrators and plural faces/hands remain
detectable for objects-only delivery. Contrast words, new subjects and later
affirmative shapes remain visible to the checks.

Invented regression controls cover lists, unknown list heads, ordinary narrator
and object shadows, EN/FR contrasts, exceptions, double negation, and an
affirmative person or silhouette after a denial. Existing invisible-phenomenon,
objects-only, silent, duration, lineage and Role2 language/schema regressions
also pass. These are source checks, not extra creative benchmark cases.

The **same six saved outputs were replayed once** under the same repaired guard
in both source arms. All six then passed the deterministic delivery checks.
The four earlier failures and initial semantic scores remain separate. A later
version-label promotion changed no executable function or writer message.
This repairs a delivery false positive; it is not a second creative generation
or a new semantic score for the framing instruction.

The guard remains lexical and conservative. For example, the postverbal denial
“The narrator is not visible” still triggers its person check; it does not
understand arbitrary negation or story meaning. Neither these checks nor a local
hash authenticate a source or validate canon, spoilers, audience comprehension
or business value. Unsupported syntax and novel semantic errors remain possible.

Full prompts, provider-derived fields, first responses and parent-attached
exports remain outside this public report. No Qloo acquisition, native model
call, run-family refill or budget reset was used for this comparison or repair.
